## About

I study how the ocean breathes and how it effects carbon and oxygen cycles in the ocean . My work combines
observational data (GLODAP, BGC-Argo, CFC-12), Lagrangian and transit-time
diagnostics, and Earth System models to quantify ocean ventilation,
anthropogenic carbon uptake, and deoxygenation.

- Ocean ventilation 
- Oxygen and carbon cycles
- Carbon overshoot and climate reversibility
- BGC-Argo data processing, QC and sensor crossover calibration
